What images drive and punctuate our journeys? What images stick with us, bind and animate us, for better or for worse?

How can images, voices and gestures intertwine, challenge and subvert colonial and xenophobic clichés while telling other stories of migration?

“The journey I looked forward” invites families facing experiences of exile to shape memories through handmade books. The workshop text brings together Brussels (erg), Johannesburg (Centre for the Less Good Idea) and Lubumbashi (PICHA).
