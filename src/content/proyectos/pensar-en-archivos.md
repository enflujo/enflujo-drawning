Workshops at the Centre for the Less Good Idea in Johannesburg, August 2022, involving, among others, Pélagie Gbaguidi, Sabine Theunissen and William Kentridge.

Participants from animation, drawing, music, performance and programming encounter archival films alongside artificial-intelligence systems, making the process of categorisation visible and available for interruption.
